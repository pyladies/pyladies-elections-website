Historia de la Junta de PyLadies

The beginnings

At PyCon US 2019, several PyLadies organizers and other key stakeholders met to discuss the future of PyLadies (minutes). After 10 years of existence, PyLadies had grown from a small collection of United States based chapters to a vibrant international community.

At the "Future of Us" workshop, participants discussed several topics including:

  • organizational priorities
  • resource management
  • fundraising

However, the largest challenge the workshop identified was the need for a centralized leadership body. Thus the Global Council was born.

To learn more about the history of the PyLadies Global Council, you can see the 2020 PyCon USA talk "9 Years of PyLadies: Lessons Learned and What Comes Next".
