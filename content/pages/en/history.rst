Histoy of the PyLadies Global Council
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The beginnings
--------------
.. container:: float-left

    .. image:: /images/about/future-of-us.png
        :width: 400px

At PyCon US 2019, several PyLadies organizers and other key stakeholders met to discuss the future of PyLadies (`minutes <https://github.com/pyladies/global-organizing/blob/master/notes/2019/06_05_2019.md>`_). After 10 years of existence, PyLadies had grown from a small collection of United States based chapters to a vibrant international community.

At the "Future of Us" workshop, participants discussed several topics including:

- organizational priorities
- resource management
- fundraising

However, the largest challenge the workshop identified was the need for a centralized leadership body. Thus the Global Council was born.

To learn more about the history of the PyLadies Global Council, you can see the 2020 PyCon USA talk `9 Years of PyLadies: Lessons Learned and What Comes Next <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KRwpY2TixAs>`_.
